The engine

Every layer of identity verification, in one container

Six analysis stages run on every document. Each contributes a weighted signal to the final verdict — so you audit why, not just what.

Forensic document analysis

Pixel-level forensics built to catch deepfakes, screen captures, and printouts: moment enhancement, error-level analysis, noise-pattern analysis, and EXIF chain inspection — rendered as heatmap overlays your analysts can actually read.

MELAELANoiseEXIFHeatmap

OCR + MRZ extraction

DocTR-powered text extraction and Machine Readable Zone parsing across 140+ document types — passports, national IDs, and driving licences.

DocTRISO 18013MRZ

Face matching

DeepFace 1:1 matching between the document face and the live capture, with cosine similarity and a configurable threshold per integration.

Liveness detection

Passive liveness cues — photometric consistency, skin texture, and ambient-light checks — to block printed photos and replayed video.

AML & sanctions screening

Fuzzy-name screening against OFAC SDN, UN, and EU lists with weighted risk scoring baked into the decision engine.

Signed webhooks & multi-tenant API

Every case completion is delivered as an HMAC-SHA256-signed webhook with automatic retries and SSRF-protected target validation. Keys are per-tenant, rotatable, and revocable — manage them from the dashboard or the API.

FAQ

Questions we get a lot

Is DeepGuard a replacement for managed vendors like Onfido or Sumsub?
Yes, functionally — it's the self-hosted equivalent. The trade-off: you own the infrastructure, updates, and scaling. What you gain: zero per-lookup cost, data sovereignty, and full access to every model output and score, not just a final flag.
Who initiates the face-verification step?
Your app does. You call the face-session endpoint, show the returned QR code in your UI, and the end user scans it on their phone to submit a live selfie. The admin dashboard is view-only by design — it displays match, liveness, and device results but never initiates the flow.
Can I plug in my own sanction lists or screening rules?
Yes. The decision engine is a weighted rule system with exposed weights and defect definitions. Sanction lists load through the screening service; rule defaults live in /api/rules/defaults and are editable at runtime.
How is my API key secured?
Keys are SHA-256 hashed before storage, per-tenant, and rotate/revoke from the dashboard. Requests are authenticated via the X-API-Key header. Rate limits and SSRF protection are on by default.
What about GDPR and data residency?
Since everything runs in your own VPC or on-prem, data never leaves your network boundary. Export endpoints let you hand your users their data on request; retention is entirely your policy to enforce.
