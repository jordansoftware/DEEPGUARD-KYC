Everything you need for compliant KYC

Built for DeFi, fintech, and any app that needs to verify identities — without a $25k/year SaaS contract.

🔬

Forensic Analysis

MELA, ELA, noise-pattern, and EXIF forensics on ID documents with pixel-level heatmap overlays to catch deepfakes, screen captures, and printouts.

📄

OCR + MRZ

DocTR-powered text extraction and Machine Readable Zone parsing for passports, national IDs, and driving licences across 140+ document types.

👤

Face Matching

DeepFace 1:1 matching between the document face and a live selfie capture, with cosine similarity scoring and configurable thresholds.

📱

Liveness Detection

Passive liveness cues (photometric consistency, skin texture, ambient light) to block printed or replayed faces.

⚠️

AML / Sanctions

Screening against OFAC SDN, UN, and EU sanction lists with fuzzy name matching and weighted risk scoring.

🔔

Signed Webhooks

HMAC-SHA256 signed webhook delivery on case completion, with automatic retries and SSRF protection on target URLs.
